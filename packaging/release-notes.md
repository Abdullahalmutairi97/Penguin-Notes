## Download

**Windows:** download **PenguinNotes-Setup.exe** below and open it. Windows may say it "protected your PC" because the app isn't signed yet: click **More info > Run anyway**.

**Linux** (Ubuntu 22.04+, Fedora, Mint, Arch, ... on 64-bit Intel/AMD): paste this in a terminal:

```
curl -fsSL https://github.com/Abdullahalmutairi97/Penguin-Notes/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh
```

or download **penguin-notes-linux-x86_64.tar.gz**, unpack it and run `sh install.sh`. No sudo needed.

## First steps

- **Pengu (AI):** open *Pengu > Settings* and pick a model: Ollama or LM Studio on your computer, or a cloud service with your API key.
- **Talk:** click *Talk*. The first time, it downloads the speech model (190 MB). After that voice works offline.
- **iPhone:** open *More > iPhone sync*. Calendar needs an Apple app-specific password. For the Notes app, pick Gmail, Yahoo or Fastmail, make an app password, and turn on Notes for that account on the iPhone.
